Community Guidelines

Last updated: June 12, 2026

Bttr Nghts is built for safe nights out with friends. These guidelines apply to parties, chats, hazard reports, profiles, and all user-generated content in the app. By using Bttr Nghts, you agree to follow them.

Be respectful

Treat others the way you would want to be treated. Do not harass, threaten, stalk, or demean other users. Hate speech, discrimination, bullying, and targeted abuse are not allowed.

No false emergencies

Do not misuse SOS or hazard reporting features. Bttr Nghts is not a replacement for emergency services. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services (for example, 911 in the United States).

Keep listings honest

Party and venue information must be accurate. Scams, impersonation, spam, and promotion of illegal activity are prohibited.

Protect privacy

Do not share someone else's personal information without their consent. Do not post content that violates another person's privacy or intellectual property rights.

Report and block

Use in-app report and block tools for parties, messages, and profiles that violate these guidelines. We review reports and may remove content, restrict features, or suspend accounts that break the rules.

Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, temporary restrictions, or permanent account suspension. Serious or repeated violations may be reported to law enforcement where appropriate.

Contact

Questions or appeals: bttrnghts@gmail.com
